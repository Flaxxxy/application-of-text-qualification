Limitations

  • Sentiment labels come from star ratings, which are noisy (e.g. "very bad" rated 3 stars).
  • Orders that combine a review and a photo are simulated; real customer photos may look different from the training photos.
  • Package photos mix real cardboard photos, product shots and 3D renders; the model is weakest on the renders.
  • The produce model knows apples, bananas and oranges only.
