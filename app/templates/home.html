Find problematic orders from what customers write and show

An online shop gets thousands of reviews a day, many with a photo. This tool reads each review, checks the photo for a damaged package or spoiled food, and combines both into one decision a support agent can act on, with the reasons spelled out.

Check an order

What the decision means

{{ status_badge("OK") }}  nothing to do

{{ status_badge("REVIEW") }}  a person should look: e.g. the photo contradicts the review, or a damage claim isn't visible

{{ status_badge("URGENT") }}  the review and the photo both show a problem

How it works

1 · Review textSentiment (negative / neutral / positive) with fine-tuned DistilBERT, plus issue tags such as "damaged packaging" or "refund request".
→
2 · Customer photoElectronics → package model (damaged vs intact). Grocery → produce model (rotten vs fresh). Both MobileNetV2.
→
3 · FusionA weighted risk score plus rules for when text and photo agree or disagree → OK / REVIEW / URGENT with reasons.
