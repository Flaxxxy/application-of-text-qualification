How it works

01

Read the review

A fine-tuned DistilBERT model rates the sentiment: negative, neutral or positive. A keyword lexicon finds concrete issues such as "damaged packaging", "wrong item" or "refund request", and understands negation ("not damaged").

02

Check the photo

The product category picks the model: electronics are checked for a damaged package, grocery for rotten produce. Both are MobileNetV2 networks, tuned to catch at least 90% of bad photos.

03

Combine and explain

A risk score plus rules for when review and photo agree or disagree, such as a happy review with a damaged box, or a damage claim with an intact box, gives one decision with its reasons.
