The business problem

An online shop receives thousands of reviews a day, many with a photo. Somewhere in that stream are damaged deliveries, spoiled food and false claims. This tool reads each review, checks the photo, and gives the support team one decision per order, with the reasons spelled out.

How it works

1Read the review
Fine-tuned DistilBERT gives negative / neutral / positive. A keyword lexicon finds issues such as "damaged packaging", "wrong item" or "refund request", and handles negation ("not damaged").
2Check the photo
The product category picks the model: electronics → damaged vs intact package, grocery → rotten vs fresh produce. Both are MobileNetV2, tuned to catch ≥ 90% of bad photos.
3Combine and explain
A weighted risk score plus rules for when review and photo agree or disagree (happy review + damaged box, damage claim + intact box) → OK / REVIEW / URGENT with reasons.
All numbers come from real test runs; the combined result uses simulated orders. Details on Models & results.
